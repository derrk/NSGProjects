What stops you is the battery, not the clock

In a cluster the sites are a few miles apart, so you burn almost nothing between them and the pack fills up. Once it is full you cannot take a real charge.

If a real session isBay Area sites in a day

This is the softest number in the whole plan. Tesla has published no minimum session size. Everything here assumes 3 kWh counts. If it turns out to need 5 kWh, the sweep days lose a third of their yield; if a plug-in of any size counts, they gain.

What this assumes

Still true from before

  • 2023 Model Y Long Range assumed. 75 kWh usable drives the sweep maths directly.
  • 43 sites already visited in 2026, all in Texas, excluded from every count here.
  • Third-party-operated sites don't score — Francis Energy, Victron and similar.
  • Community catalog, not an official Tesla feed. Re-run it close to departure.

New in this plan

  • Sweep days are routed energy-aware. When the pack fills, the next hop is deliberately longer to burn charge. That alone is worth about six sites a day over nearest-neighbour.
  • 8 minutes per stop in a metro, plus the drive between. Works out around 20 minutes per site.
  • Nothing is booked, and no hotels have been picked for this route yet.
