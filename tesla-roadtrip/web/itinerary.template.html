Assumptions

Before you go

Confirm these

  • Model Y trim. Long Range AWD assumed, 180 mi between stops in cold weather. RWD would need more stops.
  • Liberty Island tickets. Four hours is budgeted on day 5. Ferry tickets sell out at holidays and are not booked.
  • Hotels. Nine nights listed, nothing reserved. Thanksgiving week fills up, and the Friday night stop at Terrell is the one you want locked first.
  • Re-run the catalog close to departure. New sites open constantly and each one is free score.
  • Recheck the contest rules page. The start-versus-end timer wording was still contradictory on Nov 2.

What this is not

  • Not a booking, a quote, or a Tesla-confirmed result.
  • Not an EV energy model. Road distance is real; range, weather and elevation are not simulated. Check legs in the Tesla planner or ABRP.
  • Not live availability. Stall counts come from the catalog, not a live feed.
  • Sites operated by other companies on Tesla hardware are excluded from the count.
