What could still move this

The soft number

Sweep days assume a 3 kWh top-up counts as a qualifying session. Tesla has published no minimum. At 5 kWh the two sweep days would drop from 63 sites to roughly 40, and the trip total from 107 to about 84.

If Tesla clarifies this before you go, tell me and I will rerun the whole plan against the real number.

Before you leave

  • Re-pull the catalog. New sites open constantly and each is free score.
  • Recheck the contest rules. The start-versus-end timer wording was still contradictory on 2 November.
  • Arrive in the Bay low. Monday night, roll in with a near-empty pack. Tuesday's whole yield depends on having room to keep charging.
  • I-40 in late November crosses Flagstaff at 7,000 ft. Check the forecast; the southern return is your bail-out.
