The Problem

You can't answer the phone from a roof

Or from under a sink, or with a dog on the table, or with your arms in a condenser unit.

A homeowner with a leak is working down a list of search results. Your phone rings, nobody picks up, and plenty of people don't leave a voicemail — they hang up and dial the next name. By the time you've climbed down, somebody else has the job.

That is not a marketing problem. You won the search and you got the call. It was lost in the gap between the call ending and you being free to return it, and more traffic does not close a gap. The Wording

What the automatic reply should actually say

Missed-call texts fail when they read like a robot stalling. Three rules fix that. We write yours on the setup call.

👤

Say who you are

An unexplained text from an unknown number gets deleted. Lead with the business name so they know this is the company they just called.

❓

Ask one question

"Sorry we missed you" ends the conversation. "What's going on with it?" starts one. A reply is a lead you can price.

🕒

Give a real timeframe

Say when a human will call back, and make it a window you'll hit. People wait if they know how long. They won't wait on silence.
