Design That Books Work

Looking fine is not the same as booking work

Plenty of local sites look perfectly nice and still do nothing. The difference is usually one of these four.

🎯

One job per page

A page that tries to say everything gets read as nothing. Each page makes one point and asks for one thing.

📞

The phone is never far

Your number is tappable at the top, in the body, and stuck to the bottom of the screen on mobile.

💵

Prices and proof on the page

A starting price and photos of real jobs beat any stock image. People call when they know roughly what it costs.

⚡

Speed counts as design

A heavy page on a weak signal is a lost call. Light pages, compressed images, no scripts stacked in front of your headline.
