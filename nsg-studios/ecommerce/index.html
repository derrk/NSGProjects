Platform Choice

Shopify or WooCommerce?

Both sell products perfectly well. The difference is who holds the keys, where the cost lands, and whose job the maintenance is.

🛍️

Shopify

A hosted platform. Shopify runs the servers, the checkout and the security patching. You log in, add products, print labels, ship orders.

  • Checkout already built, tested and card-compliant
  • Card processing and fraud screening come with it
  • Apps for pickup, loyalty, labels and stock counts
  • Counter sales can share one inventory later
  • Trade-off: a monthly platform fee, billed to you forever
  • Trade-off: theme limits bite on deep layout changes
  • Trade-off: moving off it later means a rebuild
🧩

WooCommerce

A plugin on your own WordPress site. You pay for hosting, we install and configure. The store sits on the same site as your service pages and nothing is locked to a vendor.

  • No platform subscription — hosting is the running cost
  • Full control of layout, product fields and checkout
  • One domain carrying the store and the rest of your SEO
  • Odd product types and quote flows are straightforward
  • Trade-off: updates and backups are a real responsibility
  • Trade-off: paid plugins stack up as features are added
  • Trade-off: slower to stand up when the catalog is simple
🛍️

Shopify fits if

You are starting from nothing, the catalog is under a few hundred items, and you would rather pay a subscription than think about updates.

🧩

WooCommerce fits if

You already have a WordPress site that gets found, products need sizes, bundles or made-to-order options, or you do not want a platform fee riding on every year.

⚖️

The short answer

Shopify usually wins on time to first sale. WooCommerce usually wins on control and long-run cost. We pick one on the call and you can disagree before anything is built.

What Setup Involves

Catalog, payments, shipping, pickup

Four jobs decide whether a store works. Here is how each one gets set up.

01

Product catalog

A spreadsheet, a POS export, or photos and a handwritten list — we build the structure around your real inventory. Title, description, price, SKU, stock count and category on every item, so search and filters actually narrow things down.

02

Payments

Card payments configured and run through real test orders before launch, Texas tax applied, order alerts landing in an inbox you read. Processing fees are billed to you by the processor, not by us.

03

Shipping

Flat rate, weight-based, or live carrier rates, with box sizes and handling time set so postage does not quietly eat your margin. If you want a free-shipping threshold, we set it at a number that still pays you.

04

Local pickup

Wichita Falls customers choose pickup at checkout, pay nothing for shipping, and get a message when the order is ready. Your address and hours show on the order. Cheapest fulfillment you own, and it walks people into the store.
